Google Ecosystem / Google Workspace

Run your work on Google Workspace

Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Drive, Calendar, and Meet — connected by Gemini. This is the productivity side of the Google stack GDG Tulsa builds with, the same apps most of our members already have open every day.

The apps

Gmail

Email with AI-drafted replies, smart summaries, and privacy that's never used for advertising.

Docs

Real-time collaborative writing, now with Gemini for summarizing, drafting, and proofreading.

Drive

Cloud storage with smart search that finds files by content, owner, or type in seconds.

Calendar

Scheduling that syncs across your whole team and every other Workspace app.

Meet

Video calls with live captions, AI-generated notes, and action items pulled automatically.

NotebookLM

An AI research notebook that grounds every answer in the sources you actually upload.

All the tools you need and a few more we think you'll love.
